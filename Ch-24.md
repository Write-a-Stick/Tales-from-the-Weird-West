<h3>Chapter Twenty-Four</h3>
<h3>No good deed...</h3>
</center>

We left the cave into the sunlight, and it felt good. I was tired and emotional, the chase and delve both taking their toll. I was ready to sleep, even though it wasn't even midday. I must have looked how I felt. 

"Travis will take you home, we'll handle the rest," Decker said matter-of-factly. I didn't understand how he could be so... normal, after almost committing an atrocity I didn't even have a name for. 

_I am not ready to ask what you've seen and done, am I?_ 

"Take my bike," he continued and I was happy for my mind to shift into a different topic. _Bike. So that's what Iron Steed is really called. I like mine better._ 

I liked the plan, I really didn't want to go down again. "If you're sure you don't need me..." 

He hesitated. "Actually... can you wait for half an hour while I go down with the boys?" 

_Oof._ 

"I'm part of this now, I guess... I can do half an hour." 

We reached the rock outcropping the agents were taking cover behind and I sat down immediately, tired as I was. Everyone but Frank and me left for the cave. 

"I'll stay here and keep an eye out?" he had asked and gotten permission from Tom for. 

He stood there, leaning on the rock and cradling his rifle in the crook of his arm. _I guess I have a personal protector for now. That's nice, actually._ 

I woke up to Travis gently shaking my shoulder. "We can go." I cracked my eyes open and accepted the hand he offered to lift me up. 

We walked back toward the spot where we left the horses and Iron Steed. Everything was normal, the horses grazing on whatever greenery was within their reach. We got in, when Travis faced me. 

"Thank you," he said. "For holding Decker back. I think he would have ended up hating himself if you hadn't. Though he'd sooner die than say it." 

"You're—" I stopped. _"You're welcome" doesn't cut it._ 

I Nodded. 

We drove to Tucson, the Steed shaking somewhat on the rough terrain. There wasn't really the option to speak, with the engine rumbling as it did. I figured Travis didn't want to talk either, since he did nothing about it. 

Once home, I fed You Bastard. He pecked at the bread a few times, then jumped in his drawer roost and made himself one with the blanket. I took a long bath and went to sleep myself. 

<center>----------</center><br>

I woke up in the late afternoon, refreshed enough to face the rest of the day. My stomach was rumbling, so I went to the kitchen. Lunch was on the stove, kept still warm by the dying coals. I grabbed a plate of the stew and some bread. Coffee sounded nice too, so I went to make one as well.

Under the tin there were two envelopes, both with the same name on them.

```
Miss Emma Coates
Utopia, Eastern Avenue 3
Tucson
Arizona
```

I finished my meal, that couldn't wait. Only then I opened the first letter and took out the two pages, folded in half. I read the top one. It was on headed paper. _Fancy._

```
            ARIZONA SPECIAL INVESTIGATIONS 

Invoice for services rendered:  
1. Billable hours -$48.00  
2. Research assistance -$20.00  
3. Urgency level -$20.00  
4. Heavy equipment operation -$20.00  
```

_You're charging me for shooting that monster? Urgency? I did that!—_ 

_Hold up._ 

They were minuses, not dashes. 

```
5. Uniform damage -$5.30  
6. Non-disclosure -$25.00  
                  TOTAL:
                  -$138.30

Director
The Director

```

I looked at the other piece of paper. Also headed.

```
                FIRST BANK OF TUCSON  
This note qualifies the bearer to receive:  
One hundred and thirty-eight dollars and thirty cents  
In cash or in gold at First Bank of Tucson. 

Harold J. Foster  
Bank Manager  

```

A promissory note. I suddenly realized I was being annoyed about being paid a mint. That was more than a month's normal wages. They need to work on their naming though. _Invoice, ugh._

It was time for the other letter.

```
            ARIZONA SPECIAL INVESTIGATIONS 

Dear Miss Coates, 

Please meet The Director at your earliest convenience at  
Arizona Special Investigations headquarters in Tucson. 

On behalf of The Director  
Ben Newman 

```

I needed to talk to Travis. Decker could also answer, probably even better, but I really didn't want to see him just yet. 

I found Travis sitting in the study, glass in hand, eyes cloudy with previous drinks. 

"Emma?" he asked. "How are you?" He was slurring, just a bit. 

"Yes, it's me. I'm fine, mostly." I sat in the adjacent armchair. "You don't look great... Should I ask? And will you answer if I do?" I probed. 

He waved my concern off. "Mmmmm... No." 

I showed him the letters. "What do you think?" 

He read them slowly and carefully. "I think The Director is a right bastard. Though he seems to pay well." 

"He seems very mysterious. ASI never mentioned his existence around me. Have you met him?" 

He nodded. "You'll be fine. Just be careful what you agree to. Man can make snakes preferable to signatures. Are you going to go and see him?" 

I thought about it. "Since you think it's ok, I will. But tomorrow." 

I took a deeper breath. "I wanted to talk about what happened earlier, too. With Decker." 

"Absolutely not. It's not my story to tell. Though I will tell you that I understand the rage, every step of the way." His brow furrowed. "Now go and leave me be." 

I gave him the privacy, he clearly needed it. 

_Do I even want to know?_
