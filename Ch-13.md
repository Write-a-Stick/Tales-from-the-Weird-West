<h3>Chapter Thirteen</h3>
<h3>Literally blind</h3>
</center>

<center>----------</center><br>

The next shift was normal, until someone asked me for a beer we didn't stock, but I was sure we did. I tried finding it, but my vision blured, and I couldn't read any of the labels. Once we closed, I felt my body become hot... I ran a hand down my hip, only to realize I was standing naked behind the bar in full view of a throng of an audience.

‘...CADOODLEDOOOOO!’

I jumped straight up in bed, as would anyone oh so tenderly woken up by You Bastard.

'You Bastard!' I shouted back at him, my heart right up in my throat and my right ear ringing. Just as he nuzzled his head against my chin.

'Bwaak!' He fluttered his wings, then jumped on the table where I kept the sack with his grains. He pecked the table next to it with the energy of 'Can I see your manager?'

I felt like had I almost jumped out of my nightgown.

_Is that how you get scared shirtless?_

I wiped my eyes and got out of bed.

'I guess I should thank you for cutting that dream short.' I felt my ears burn, suddenly. I threw a handful of seeds to the floor corner, not enough to fill him. I made up the difference with a biscuit treat from the cupboard.

I leaned down and passed the cracker to You Bastard.

'So... Thanks.' My words were drowned in the flurry of pecks that obliterated the biscuit.

I got dressed and went down to the kitchen for a coffee. I took the Merry Bunch book with me, hoping to sneak in a few chapters. I barely had time to read the title before. Blood Hunger. Chilling. 

>'... the bloodsuckers must be creating the storms to obscure the sun.' The Hedge Wizard guessed.  
>'How do they do it though?' Asked Squirrelman.  
>'I'm not sure, but Psittacus is a smart bird, maybe he can detect the centre from how the air moves?' The Swashbuckler sent the wise parrot up.  

I always thought it was interesting that none of the Merry bunch characters had actual names, just titles. I eventually figured out it must be for the reader to easier insert themselves into the story. Some animals had names, like the wise parrot Psittacus.

Three knocks on the door. I was so engrossed in the book, I opened the door without thinking. Anyone from the household would just open the door themselves. In this case, You Bastard strode in, jumped on a stool and watched me.

'What's gotten into you today?'

'Pock BWAAAK!' Like heck was I going to rely on a rooster's feelings in my life decisions. I went back to reading. The story was gripping, The Bunch planning to break out the Weather Witch held by the vampires. Just as Squirrelman was about to deliver a letter to her, assuring her of her little brother's safety, the door opened and a sleepy Tiffany walked in and poured herself a coffee from the pot.

'Morning... What are you reading?' She asked groggily and I showed her the book cover.

'How far in are you?' She asked sharply. I didn't understand what got her so agitated.

'Umm... Squirrelman is about to deliver a message to the Weather Witch.' Tiffany went quiet for a long moment, her forehead furrowing in thought.

'I hate that name.' She finally said.

'Why? I think it holds the theme pretty well and has a good ring to it...' She tilted her head, raised an eyebrow, clearly waiting for something.

_What? It's just a book about...  
a group of misfits, saving a teenage girl from captivity...  
held there by her love for her little brother...
OH  
NO  
IMPOSSIBLE_

'Yes.' The Weather Witch said and nodded sadly.

_Robbie's stash._  
_Tiffany not able to sleep with strangers around._  
_These books are never going to be as fun to read again..._

I stood up and embraced her without asking. I needed to do it for her benefit and mine, in equal measure. She stiffened, but slowly relaxed.

'You should finish the book sometime.' She finally said.

_I will be the best fake sister I can. I promise._
