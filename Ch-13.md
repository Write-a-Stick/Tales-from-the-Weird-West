It wasn't until after the sun set, when I had to assert myself in control of the place. I had just emptied my tray of dirty glasses, when Mr. Richardson, one of the regulars, decided he had enough to drink to fancy me.

'Emma, why don't you sit down and keep me company?' He slurred, but I was fluent in Mumblish.

'I'm working, Mr. Richardson, I can't. Sorry.' _Not sorry, find a woman your age._

Had I been on my side of the bar, he wouldn't have had the chance, but as it was, he somehow decided that putting his hand on my chest was a great idea.

Been there, done that. I snapped my tray up and knocked his hand to the side before it even got close.

'Ahh, apologies Mr. Richardson. You startled me there.' I said as I watched him rub the undoubtedly painful mark the side of the tray made. 'I think you might have had enough for tonight. Please come again.' _Please don't._

It turns out Mr. Richardson wasn't one of the reasonable drunks.

'You hit me, you little whore!' He shouted, spittle flying towards my face. I took a step back as he raised a fist to hit me. I was terrified, that wasn't something I was used to. I instinctively covered myself with the tray and the impact was enough to push me back a little, accompanied by a crunch and a pained scream.

'I'll kill you!' He lunged at me. _Oh fuck oh fuck oh fuck._

It was a good thing I've seen drunken fights far too many times, I could see how he was going to move. I remember everything slowing down and turning a bit gray as I shifted my grip on the tray and smacked him over the head with it. I distinctly remember the beautiful curve of the arc the tray made, and the empty, wooden sound of the impact.

_I wonder if it's normal._

I couldn't explain it, but it didn't feel normal to be thinking so many things, so quickly, while scared shirtless.

I was standing there in silence, breathing heavily, all eyes turned to me. I saw Jim was halfway here, presumably to get Richardson out, judging by how he took his hand from under his coat. He straightened himself and looked over the unconscious man at our feet. I noticed half dozen other men in the room relaxed visibly and moved their hands off their hips.

'I'll take care of this, get him to a doctor. You sit down, Emma.'

The bar slowly settled, then Tiffany came to me with a glass of iced water and an Atherton.

'I'll take care of the bar for now. Are you ok?'

'I'm fine. I think. Give me five minutes.'

I downed the shot and settled my nerves, sipping the cold water. I went back behind the bar, having seen Jim and two other men carry Richardson out.

'Why didn't you tell me Richardson is so handsy?' I fired at Tiffany.

'He's not! He is always polite, I've known him for about a year now.'

'Did he drink a lot more today than normal?'

'No, he's a heavy drinker, he handles it well, normally.' 

'Tiffany, do you think this is something we should tell Travis or Decker about?'

'Absolutely, but I hope it's nothing important.'

Well, she was wrong.
