I didn't finish the book. It was no longer the fun read it was before. I was sure I could still enjoy them, but the experience will be forever tainted by the thought my cheap entertainment was once someone's worst day...

Not long after Tiffany, Travis came into the kitchen from outside.

'Morning.' He greeted us as soon as he entered.

'Morning.'

'Morning.'

'Bwaaak.'

The man barely raised an eyebrow at a chicken in his kitchen. He poured himself a mug of coffee and a saucer for You Bastard. The chicken looked at the brown liquid suspiciously, but gave it a chicken sip. I tore off a piece of bread and put it in his coffee to soak. I wanted to see if he liked it, too.

'Let it soak, first' I told him.

Lo and behold, You Bastard started watching the browning bread patiently. All three of us sipped our own and watched the whole thing with fascination. As soon as the liquid was absorbed, he ate it in just a few pecks.

'Bwaaak. Bock bock.' He clucked, satisfied.

'I like it too.' I told him.

Travis set down his finished coffee and looked at me.

'Emma, you should go to the clinic and see Dr. Endaway.'

'Why? I feel fine. Do I look sick?' I looked around.

'No, but you were in a scrap yesterday. I heard a tray was involved. Just to be sure.'

'I'm fine. It's a waste of money.' I replied.

'You should go.' That was Tiffany. 'Travis is just being careful. You should be too.'

'Fine. When?' I turned to Travis.

'Is now good?'

'No, but might as well get it out of the way. I'll see you all later.'

I got up, went upstairs to get my coat, You Bastard following right behind me, basically at my heel.

'Stay.' I pointed a finger at him.

'BWAAK Pock!'

'You and your bad feelings. Stop being so dramatic. I'll be back soon.'

'BWAAK Pock!' He positioned himself right next to the door, waiting for me to open it.

'No. You stay.'

'Bock Cadaa.' The stubborn little menace just scratched the floor in refusal.

Time for tricks. I took a treat from the cupboard and threw it in the centre of the room. He launched himself after it, just to realize I was disappearing behind the door.

'BWAAAK! BWAAAK! BWAAAK!' He made a tremendous racket, so I quickly dashed outside to avoid unnecessary questions. As I started toward the clinic, I could still hear You Bastard's angry noises.

'...aak'

I didn't even get to the corner when a triumphant cry reached my ears.

'Bock BWAAK!'

I turned around to see You Bastard wildly flapping his wings to slow his fall as he came towards me, and a smiling Tiffany waving from the open window. You Bastard landed, ran up to me and pecked me in the knee.

'Ow! You Bastard!' I scolded him.

'Bock BWAAK!'

'Fine, if it makes you feel better. But be quiet.'

'Bock bock!' Small wins.

I reached Dr. Endaway's office after a short negotiation with the clinic receptionist regarding their 'No animals' policy. Which, it turned out, was much easier than getting You Bastard to stay at home. The door was adorned with a polished brass plaque reading:

> Dr. R. Endaway  
> Associate Surgeon

I knocked and entered, once prompted. The office was quite large, with a desk and bookshelf taking most of the space at the front, where Dr. Endaway was sitting. He looked up and greeted me professionally.

'Miss Coates, good morning. Please sit. Thank you for coming.' He pointed at the armchair opposite him. I took the seat while You Bastard hopped on the armrest.

'Good morning, Dr. Endaway.' He barely raised an eyebrow at the rooster.

'Mr. Peckersley, good morning.'

'Bwaak.'

_Huh?_

Those two had met before. And I never knew.

_Normal doctor's appointment, Emma. Normal doctor's appointment._

He started asking me questions rapid fire, starting and filling a new page in his leatherbound journal.

'Name? Please include middle names.' - 'Emma Coates, no middle names.'
'Date of birth?' - 'September 13th, 1861'
'What sicknesses or injuries have you had before?' - I told him, nothing special, I broke my arm once.

Normal questions, until they weren't.

'Do you hear voices?'

'Do you have repeating dreams?'

'Are you experiencing any unusual cravings?'

I was happy to answer 'No.' to all of them.

'What do you see here?' He showed me a black blot on a piece of paper.

'A collision between an inkwell and an elbow.'

'I see. Literal.' He noted something down. 'Please go behind that screen there and remove your overgarments and shoes.' I did as asked, with You Bastard following closely. Luckily, this was just a doctor's examination and I didn't need to be naked for it. A welcome note after the dream I had.

When I was ready, he took my measurements similar to how a tailor would, then noted them down. He weighed me and listened to my heart.

Suddenly the door burst open and a nurse rushed in.

'Dr. Endaway! We have another one on the way, they say it's bad.'

'I'm coming.' He turned to me. 'Miss Coates, please remain here, we aren't finished. This shouldn't take too long.'

With that, he left the room and I decided to get dressed.

I had nothing to do, so I looked around the room a bit more carefully. The furniture was good and the tools that were visible remained mostly unrecognizable to me. Then I noticed the abandoned notebook on the desk.

_Just a peek._

I looked around to make sure I was still alone and quickly started reading the open page. Perfectly normal things written in a neat cursive:

> Observing physician: Dr. Roscoe Endaway.  
> Subject: Emma Coates, human female.  
> DoB: September 13th, 1861.  
> Previous illnesses: Right arm broken at age 12, fully healed.  

And then not so normal:

> Classification: Identified Outist, unafilliated.
> Birthsign: Virgo  
> Chinese Birthsign: Metal Rooster  
> Potential threat level: Pending assessment.  
>
> Event Tucson-4 diagnosis:  
> No voices  
> No repeating dreams  
> No craving for human flesh  
> Subject did not appear excessively emotional.  

_What is this? 'Just in case', Travis? 'Please remain here', is it?_

I flipped the pages at random, when the word 'vampire' appeared.

>July 15th, 1879. 
>
>Presiding physician: Dr. Roscoe Endaway.
>Subject: Charles Mikaelson, confirmed ‘vampire’. Visually human male.
>Classification: Supernatural predator, shapeshifter, exsanguinator.
>Threat assessment: Moderate to high.
>
>The subject looks and behaves like a human male of age around 30. 
>
>Recovered anecdotal and physical evidence hints at:
>1.A lifespan of hundreds of years.  
>2.Vampirism in subject’s strain originates from a deal with the Devil or another demon from Hell.  
>3.Ability to shift shape into quieter or deadlier forms.  
>
>Subject when interrogated, was resistant to:  
>1.Steel inflicted lacerations - heal rapidly.  
>2.Blunt trauma - heals rapidly, but can be used to sedate the subject if used in repetition.  
>3.Running water - no effect.  
>4.Garlic - subject did not enjoy the smell.  
>5.Unwarded Thresholds.  
>6.Invitation-dependent mythos.  
>  
>Subject displayed vulnerability to the following:  
>  
>1.Silver - wounds self-cauterize and do not heal.  
>2.Fire - burn wounds do not heal.  
>3.Holy water - has a burning effect, wounds do not heal.  
>4.Decapitation. Note: Mr McCoy displays callous disregard for the ‘life’ of vampires. I do not disagree.  
>  
>The head contains a black liquid, travelling through the subjects brain independently of fluid dynamics. Evidence inconclusive, but suggests it is the carrier and enabler of this strain of vampirism. Burn before contact. Separate and monitor affected individuals.
>
>Recommendation: 
>
>Identify and terminate.

This was fascinating. Mortifying, but fascinating. 

I looked around to make sure I haven't been discovered and flipped some more pages, trying to find something else that made sense. A lot of it were either short snippets, or quick drawings, scribbled half-sentences. Then a page caught my eye:

> Observing physician: Dr. Roscoe Endaway.  
> Subject: Archibald Alfonse Deckard Cotterill-McCoy III, AKA ‘Decker McCoy’, human male.  
> DoB: Unconfirmed, appears around 35.
> Classification: Known Outist. Shamanic powers witnessed. Associations undisclosed.  
> Potential threat level: Catastrophic.  
>
> Mr. McCoy at first presents as a clueless socialite, unaware or uninterested in regular conventions. He usually responds to inquiry into personal matters with deflection, humour and assigning excessive importance to looks and fashion. With most people it seems to create a sort of filter, that makes him noticeably invisible - for lack of a better term.
>
>I have observed Mr. McCoy in a variety of situations 

'Cabwaak?' You Bastard abruptly interrupted my reading. He was walking in a circle, almost spinning. 'Cabwaak?'

'What's wrong?' I asked him.

'BWAAAK! Pock.' I didn't feel anything. But I started hearing agitated, loud voices coming from the outside.

'Hold him down!'

'He's too strong!'

'Then break his arm!'

I went to the window just to hear a slightly muffled, pained howl coming from the wagon just arriving in front of the clinic.

Six men dragged a kicking, screaming, ropebound man inside. 

A man dressed in blue.

A man I knew.

I ran to the door and cracked it open slightly, just so I could see. Coming up the stairs were 6 dirty, bruised, crumpled and tired looking men, holding a thrashing Mr. Song between them. They were being ushered into the adjacent room by Dr. Endaway.

This exact moment You Bastard pushed the door, slipped through the opening and dashed at the group, inflating quickly along the way.

'Cockadoo! Cockadoo!'

And finally, after landing on Mr. Song's chest...

'CADOODLEDOOOOO!' He crowed in his face.

Somehow the whole group of men tumbled like pupeteered sacks of potatoes with their strings cut.

'Whomever has their leg on my arm, would you kindly remove it? It's bending the wrong way in one too many places.'

We all looked at the very calm Mr. Song, who was petting You Bastard's head, while lying in a heap on the floor.

'I take it you are You Bastard?'

'Bock bock.'
