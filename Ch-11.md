<h3>Chapter Eleven</h3>
<h3>I see tea</h3>
</center>

_Something's off..._

A shiver went down my spine. I opened my eyes wide and took in the room. Extremely carefully this time.

A man from the table across made eye contact with me, the nosy bastard. He even had the audacity to smile. 

_What are you looking at me for?! Can't you see I'm busy?!_

The surroundings looked the same under scrutiny. Wooden walls, tables with chairs around them. Tiffany sitting in hers, frozen. Decker eyeing everything from under knitted eyebrows, including the chair between Tiffany and me. 

_Nothing to see here, four chairs, four iced teas._

I started looking out the window.

_FOUR?!_

I snapped my head to the empty chair and my stomach dropped. I suddenly saw the strange man in blue in the not-so-empty chair. Hands resting on his cane, lively brown eyes smiling at us over the fourth glass.

The others must have seen him too. Tiffany suddenly shifted herself away, while Decker focused intensely and leaned forward. He also made a shrugging motion that ended with a quiet but ominous click coming from under the table.

The intruder's posture remained unchanged, but his eyes widened for an instant, before settling into a smile again.

_I guess his magic doesn't stop bullets. Good to know._

'Greetings to you all. I am Tie Song.' He pronounced it "tea-eh". 'Means 'Iron Pine' in your language.' He said politely, as the feeling of wrongness dissipated. 

I wasn't going to let that distract me. I watched him like a hawk, not letting him out of my sight. Decker's voice came from my left.

'I don't appreciate people sneaking up on me like that, Mr Sung. Give me one reason I shouldn't give you a few high speed silver keepsakes?'

'Because I am not your enemy? I had every opportunity when you turned around.' The old man's smile turned from friendly to a knowing one. 

He wasn't wrong, you can't defend yourself from an attack you don't know is coming, yet we all continued breathing through the usual number of holes. The conclusion was pretty obvious.

McCoy put his gun away with another click.

'Also, it is __Song__. Tie __SONG.__' He added with the inflection and expression of someone who made the correction a thousand times already and expects another thousand soon. 

'Apologies then, Mr. __SONG__. What do you want from me? I assume you know who I am?'

'From you? Nothing. I don't know you, I came for The Rooster.' He replied. 

McCoy's expression was somewhere between surprise, relief and offense. You Bastard being the target of some kind of plot was too ridiculous not to ask about, though.

'You want... You Bastard? He's just a chicken!' I faced the old man, staring in disbelief.

'What chicken?' He looked genuinely confused.

'You Bastard... the rooster. You're the one who brought him up!' Mr. Song's face went blank, then turned into a grin.

'You have... hahaha... a chicken, called __You Bastard__? A rooster?' I nodded, I thought that was obvious.

'America never ceases to amaze...' He shook his head in dismay. 'No, I came here for The Rooster. You.' He looked me in the eyes. 'You are nineteen, yes?'

'I am, but what does this have to do with anything? As you can see, I am not a bird.' I was done being interrogated and treated like the village idiot. This whole mysterious stranger routine started being just that. Routine. I turned the tables on him. 'It seems you know things about me, and I barely know your name, Mr. Song. I am sick and tired of people appearing in my life with an eclectic collection of questions, warnings and mysterious sayings. Why don't you tell me what you want?'

Mr. Song pondered for a while, then nodded.

'I think you recently... how do you say... stopped sleeping? Became alert?... No...' His bushy brows furrowed in concentration. 

'Awakened?' I couldn't help but jump in.

'YES! Awakened!' He said a fair bit too loudly, clapped and pointed at me. I noticed other patrons of the cafe pointedly ignoring our existence. No magic, purely a social reaction.

'I'm going to assume you do not mean the time I got out of bed today.' I couldn't help myself. Also, my turn to be vague. 'Something of the sort, yes. How do you know?'

'I feel it. Your Qi is unfocused. It swirls around you like an animal.' 

_Qi. Now I have another name for it._

'I know that much. How about you tell me something I don't know?' I fired back.

'You are untrained, yet you saw me.'

'I did, and?'

'No one else has. Your Qi knew before you did. Also, you are going to have a guest.' He grinned and nodded his head toward the opening door. 

A man wearing an ASI badge headed straight to our table with a sense of urgency, taking a good look at us as he approached.

'Good afternoon, ladies.' He then ignored us and looked straight at Decker. 'B... Decker, there's been a development. Operations wants to see you.'

McCoy faced Mr. Song, who was nowhere to be seen, just a half empty glass of iced tea abandoned on the table. 

_Of course. As soon as I was starting to get somewhere._

'Bastard...' Decker wasn't happy either.

Tiffany tapped the empty chair cushion gingerly.

'Still warm. I don't like him.'

The new arrival looked confused, but no less agitated than when he came in.

'McCoy? Are you coming? Special rate.'

'That bad, huh? Fine. Get these two to Utopia and I'll go speak to Ops.' He put down a $5 bill, got up and left us there.

'Got it. Ladies, Thomas Brine, at your service.' The young armed man smiled and looked at us expectantly. Tiffany and I exchanged glances.

'Emma Coates, pleasure to meet you, Mr. Brine.' I stood up. Never hurts to be polite. 'But we can find our own way back.'

'I was told. It seemed like you were too.' He countered.

'I guess we should? Also, Tom isn't going to let go.' Tiffany added, and Tom tilted his hat in acknowledgement.

_Tom? I guess she knows him._

I said my goodbyes to the servers. I wanted to come here again at some point.

It was still warm outside, a light wind at our backs as we traversed the busy streets. 

I wanted to talk to Tiffany about Mr. Song, but with the ASI man there, it wasn't much of an option. I didn't see the old man anywhere, nor did the feeling of wrongness return. I was still expecting him to pop out of nowhere, but at least he wasn't an immediate threat.

Unlike whatever Decker was called away to. Also, now that I had a minute to think, I revisited McCoy's exchange with Brine. It was a vague exchange of information that only those with more context could really understand. I decided to see if I could pull more of that.

'Mr. Brine, what were you going to call Mr. McCoy? B-something?' I watched him intently when I mentioned the stumble. He didn't expect to be called out, I saw uncertainty flash across his face. Though truth be told, I could have missed it entirely if I wasn't watching so closely.

'Nothing, miss. Just a slip of the tongue.' He went stonefaced, I could see he was now guarded. I assumed it was typical for criminals, but I wasn't an expert. Also, between what I knew of Decker and saw of this man, it didn't feel right.

'I see, thank you.' We reached Utopia mostly in silence, awkward and not-so-companionable.

'Ladies.' Brine tipped his hat, turned and went his way. 

We went in to see the room in that post-lunch lull. A few people were finishing their drinks while Bonnie was clearing tables.

'Tom not coming in?' She asked.

'No, probably had something to do. Decker went to ASI.' Tiffany filled her in, but there was browish mixed in the exchange. It seemed I was the only one unfamiliar with the man.

'So who is Tom, exactly? Looks like you two know him?' I asked. Bonnie delivered.

'Beer and whiskey. Fussy about beer. Usually one and one after work. Works for ASI, obviously. Never let him drink alone, he'll crush you with philosophy.' Let no-one ever say Bonnie isn't a bartender through-and-through.

'And what aren't you telling me?'

'Nothing!' Tiffany jumped in instantly. I immediately became suspicious, then saw her ears and cheeks flush.

_Oh..._

I didn't smile or laugh. Not at all, not in the slightest. But it was hard. I needed to extricate myself from the situation before I slipped. 

'Fine, keep your secrets!' I stormed off through the back door, into the house.

_If and when you're ready, Tiffany._
