_Something's off..._

Shivers went down my spine, I opened my eyes wide and took in the room. Extremely carefully this time.

A man from the table across made eye contact with me, the nosy bastard. He even had the audacity to smile. 

_What are you looking at me for?! Can't you see I'm busy?!_

The surroundings looked the same under scrutiny. Wooden walls, tables with chairs around them. Tiffany sitting in hers, frozen. Decker eyeing everything from under knitted eyebrows, including the chair between Tiffany and me. 

_Nothing to see here, four chairs, four ice teas._

I started looking out the window.

_FOUR?!_

I snapped my head to the empty chair and my stomach dropped. I suddenly saw the strange man in blue in the not-so-empty chair. Hands resting on his cane, lively brown eyes smiling at us over the fourth glass.

The others must have seen him too. Tiffany suddenly shifted herself away, while Decker focused intensely and leaned forward. He also made a shrugging motion that ended with a quiet, but ominous click coming from under the table.

The intruder's posture remained unchanged, but his eyes widened for an instant, then settled into a calm mask again.

_I guess his magic doesn't stop bullets. Good to know_

'Greeting to you all. I am Tie Song. 'Iron Pine' in your language.' He said politely, as the feeling of wrongness dissipated. 

I wasn't going to let that distract me. I watched him like a hawk, not letting him out of my sight. Decker's voice came from my left.

'I don't appreciate people sneaking up on me like that, Mr Song. Give me one reason I shouldn't give you a few high speed silver keepsakes?'

The old man just smiled.

'Because I am not your enemy? I had all opportunity when you turned around.' I realized the smile wasn't polite, it was a knowing one. He wasn't wrong, he sat between us for who knows how long? Probably came in with us.

'Alright Mr Song, fair...' McCoy put his gun away back into the holster, staring our companion down. 'What do you want from me?'

'From you? Nothing. I came for the Wakened Rooster.' He replied. I couldn't understand it, so I asked.'

'You want... You Bastard? He's just a chicken! Well, mostly...'

He chuckled at that and looked directly at me now.

'No, silly girl. You are ten and nine winters, yes? You see the layers of the world? Your master guided you through your Awakening?'
