<h3>Chapter Eighteen</h3>
<h3>Of all the colors</h3>
</center>

I had never been to a laboratory, field or otherwise, so I had no expectation of what one should look like. What I saw looked like something that shouldn't. Namely, a pair of seemingly abandoned train cars in a forest clearing, a mile outside of town and away from any train tracks. The two steel cars sat on the ground, supported from both sides by wide, angled steel plates. Both had long canvas "skirts" that covered the space between the undercarriage and the ground. 

A half dozen ASI agents patrolled the area, armed with shotguns and rifles. They looked tired, but ready. I managed to notice a few ravens perched in the trees or flying slowly overhead. While I couldn't be sure, these were probably Decker's feathery spies.

We disembarked the wagon, Mr. Song was carried out on a stretcher. You Bastard followed at my heel. 

I didn't feel fully safe, this place wasn't very welcoming, somehow. Like I was being watched from the trees. It wasn't the ravens.I started looking around for the source of my unease as we were being led toward the laboratory. 

"BWAAK! Pock!" You Bastard flapped his wings hard and crowed repeatedly, getting underfoot. "BWAAK! Pock!" He looked extremely distressed. "BWAAK! Pock!"

The three other roosters joined from the cages they were carried in. "BWAAK! Pock! BWAAK! Pock! BWAAK! Pock!" The racket became unbearably loud and distracting.

"Quiet, You!" I pointed at You Bastard with no hope of success, but I had to try.

Lo and behold, silence...

"Huh?" I pronounced eloquently. I stood there for a second, processing, while You Bastard tried to push me backward with his head and flapping wings. "What's wrong?" I knelt to face him.

"BWAAK! Pock!"

"Yes, bad feeling, ok."

"Bock Cadaa Bwaaak."

"No... Hello?"

"Bock Cadaa _Bwaaak_"

"No good. I see."

"Bock Cadaa! Bock Cadaa!" He jumped in my arms and pointed toward the leftmost car with his whole body, glaring at it.

"No? No, what?" I was already not very comfortable in this place and he wasn't making it any better.

_Something's off..._

I started looking around, very carefully. The first thing I noticed was that all eyes were on me.

_What are you all looking at?!_

Their incessant eyes were drilling into me, I felt righteous fury at this violation of my privacy. I covered myself with my hands to preserve the little modesty I still had left.

"Stop watching!" I blurted at them at the top of my lungs.

About half of them turned around immediately, while I started glaring at the rest, furious and embarassed at being seen this way. The longer I glared, the more people stopped watching.

Dr. Endaway approached and showed me a sort of rigid hood. "Miss Coates, I'm going to put this on you. Try to not focus on people watching you." 

"Don't touch me!" I turned away from most of the people, swiped the hood from his hand and put it on myself. 

It limited my vision to front only. I immediately felt so much of the pressure dissipate. I could no longer see my nakedness being observed from all sides.

_Wait... I'm not naked... Why did I think I was naked?_

As soon as the fury dissipated enough and I could think clearly again, I started noticing. 

The tied up Mr. Song. The leftmost car. 

About six feet over Mr. Song's head, I saw what looked like a long, dangling tube made of smoke. My eyes followed it upward, a long, long way up. At the other end of the tube, there was a sort of black bulb, also made of this smoke, changing shape and undulating. There were four other bulbs over the leftmost car, the tentacles reaching in right through its roof. These looked smaller than the one over Mr. Song, shrivelled, even. And the tentacles looked thinner.

They were all disgusting... The more I looked at them, the more they looked like... they were made of not-flesh, not-smoke. Like... there existed an absence of these two specific things there.

Tom Brine broke me away from my observations. "Miss Coates, what do you see?" I was thankful for it, as eye-drawing and ugly-fascinating those things were.

"It's... there's... like black jellyfish made of... uh... fleshy smoke that's not there? With a single tentacle." I looked around to confirm. "One has a tentacle six feet over Mr. Song, and four are reaching into the leftmost... rail car let's call it."

Then I remembered Travis's warning, from the day of the magic lecture. "Black is the color of ancient, indescribable creatures born of the Abyss between worlds. If you see black, run."

_Black. I should run. Right now._

"You look ready to bolt, Miss Coates," Tom mentioned casually, but looked at me very intensely.

Fear, curiosity, disgust and anger were bubbling inside me, threatening to spill. I felt this whirlwind once before and that time it ended with something I would rather forget.

_Not again... No..._ 

_Breathe._ 

_In..._ 

_...Out._ 

"I... it's black. I was told to avoid black at all costs and immediately," I said.

"Alright." He took out a small notebook and pencil and started writing something in it. "I need you to peek every so often, even if it scares you. I need to know if something starts happening." He turned to Dr. Endaway. "Doctor, this is your realm."

"Thank you," Endaway replied.

What followed was a session of looking and describing, until the doctor had drawn a fairly accurate image what was-not there. Endaway had a good hand, everyone who saw the drawing recoiled.

"We need to have you see what's inside, Miss Coates," he said. 

We started moving toward the leftmost car.

"BWAAAK!" You Bastard got underfoot and wildly flapped his wings, as if trying to shoo me away from that direction. "BWAAAK!"

The other roosters immediately joined him in the racket. The hood helped with things I could see, not hear. It immediately became too much, too quickly. I couldn't hear my own thoughts. I really needed space to think, so I covered my ears and closed my eyes tight.

_Please be quiet! Pleasebe quiet!! Pleasebequiet!!! PLEASEBE..._

"...QUIET!!!!" I shouted at You Bastard.

Silence. 

I could think again. Just like that.

But the Roosters didn't stop. They still flapped their wings and opened their beaks. In the silence, people were looking around and putting fingers in their ears. Kind of like when some water got in them buring a bath. Eventually the roosters caught up and started looking just as confused as the people.

"..." I said.

"...?" Tom Brine.

"...!" Dr. Endaway.

It was quite a funny thing to see, everyone trying to communicate soundlessly. Or maybe it was the relief flooding through me. Regardless, sound came back with a pop.

"Cabwaak?" You Bastard asked.

"I'm not sure." I knelt in front of him. "But I need you to be quiet, okay? Too much noise hurts."

"Cabwaak? Dak Dak BWAAAK?"

"Yes, sometimes singing bad."

He scratched in the ground furiously. "Dak dak bock cadaa BWAAK!" Of course he was going to be stubborn about it.

"I need to go there and try and help," I told him.

"Boot boot prr?" he asked.

"No... not flock. But help like flock."

You Bastard blinked a few times, then clearly came to some sort of conclusion. "Bock bock!" He ran to the cages with the other three roosters and started pecking at the bars and clucking to those inside. "Bwaaak, boot boot prr! BWAAK pock! BWAAK pock, Dak Dak!"

The slack-jawed Frank opened the cages. He clearly wanted to see what happened just as much as I did.

Once released, You Bastard was joined by the other three, and I suddenly gained an honour guard. They made a few steps toward the rail car, when You Bastard looked back at me with "You coming?!" in his eyes.

We took the steps up to the car in question. As I was approaching, I forced myself to look at the monsters. They were still hovering there, just like the one over Mr. Song.

_Mr. Song!_

I looked back in panic, only to notice nothing has changed, and he was still fine. The tentacle hovered no lower than before.

I gathered myself and went past the first metal door, into the small room beyond it. Empty, there was just one other door. This one had a little window in it, the glass protected on both sides by steel grating.

I took a few slow steps and looked through it. There were barred cells on each side, four of which were occupied. One woman and three men, Mr. Richardson among them. Each laid in a bed, motionless, as if they were sleeping. To each of their foreheads a tentacle was connected. Or rather, it looked more like the tentacle happened to go inside their heads.

A shudder went through me at the sight.

I was furious with Richardson, but seeing him in this state, it felt petty.  
This was more important.  
I was still offended.  
I would happily take out my frustration on him once he was fine.  

_I really, really, REALLY hope it ends well..._
