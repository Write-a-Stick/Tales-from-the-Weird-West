<h3>Chapter Twenty-Three</h3>
<h3>Bite of past mistakes</h3>
</center>

We rode slowly for a few more minutes, when Decker ordered us off. I got out of the side car, while the others jumped off their horses. Frank immediately approached to take Wraithbreaker off the mount. 

"I'll carry it for you." He smiled and slung it over his shoulder. He did not forget to take the box of shells too. 

I was grateful, that thing was really bulky and unwieldy. "Thank you." I smiled back at him, not like he wasn't doing me a favor. 

I just stood there, while everyone was checking and loading weapons, hitching horses or praying. It was clearly a well practiced routine for all of them, and by extension it meant it wasn't going to be safe in the slightest. 

Once he was done, Frank put a sawn off shotgun in my hands and handed me a handful of shells. "Loaded with silver. Guaranteed bad time for anyone on the receiving end. Just in case." He smiled again. "Don't forget to brace well, it still kicks." By comparison, it looked more like a toy, but I was grateful for the reminder. 

I put the shells in the Adventure Bag and held the weapon pointing away from people. I could see why this was so important. Hold it wrong, trip and disaster ready. Right now nothing was going to happen, but I remember my hands shaking when danger was close. Not to mention long after the fact. And if anything were to happen, it would be close. 

_I see why you were watching for bad habits, Decker._ 

In a few minutes everyone was ready, we veered off the previous direction, up along a dry stream bed. We followed it mostly in silence. No one was talking, only Tom or Decker were giving instructions, while others obeyed. There were also hand signals. It felt quite military, I was a fish out of water here. Somehow I was also making more noise than the much heavier and bigger men around me. 

"Watch that rock, good for ambush." 

"Bushes. Right." 

"Treeline." 

As we got closer to our destination, I started seeing wooden signs here and there. They were bright red, sometimes faded, but all had black writing stencilled on them. 

```
Private land 
By authority of Arizona Special Investigations 
Entrance is forbidden under threat of death. 

Violations will be punished to the full extent of the law. 
```

"Tom, what's that about?" I whispered. I just had to ask.

He moved closer and kept his voice down too. "It's a dangerous place, we don't want people wandering in and getting killed or disappeared. The signs are usually ominous enough," he responded. "Though it's been silent and dormant for a couple of years now." 

"Correct. And now someone's poking at it again," Decker added. "We'll be going underground. Now quiet, we're getting close." 

He wasn't lying, the stream bed turned and as we cleared another rocky outcrop, a dark cave entrance appeared about a hundred yards in front of us. 

Tom started giving orders. "We'll set up here, good cover, good distance and the ground is open."

"You're not coming?" I asked, surprised. 

"No, we won't be any use. It's a job for big boys and girls," he said. 

I looked around. Only Decker, Travis and I were left. Tom, and the other agents took positions and glared outward into our surroundings. Only Frank was left, waiting to hand off Wraithbreaker.

"Decker, what is this?" I asked, perhaps slightly too loud for the circumstances. 

"They won't see what they need to see inside, and also light would give us away. I don't want whomever or whatever is there to know we're coming," he explained. 

"Just the three of us? In there? No way." 

"Better odds than we normally get, right, Travis?" 

"I hate you. I have absolutely no idea why keep being friends with you." Travis grinned, in spite of his words. 

_I don't like it, three is too few. We're going to get killed in there._ 

My heart was thumping in my chest and I could feel terror crawling up my spine. I couldn't shake the feeling the cave entrance was watching me. I took a step back, looking for an escape route. 

I felt a weight on my shoe and heard a comforting "Boot boot prr." 

The collective reaction I could see was surprise and incredulity at the level of stealth You Bastard was apparently capable of. 

I, for one, was happy to see him. Somehow, having him here made the cave seem less threatening. 

McCoy looked at me for a long while. "As long as he's quiet, you can take him with," he finally said. 

_Please be silent, pleasepleaseplease._ 

I wasn't sure what to expect, so just in case I picked You Bastard up and put him in the Adventure bag, head poking out from within. "Shhhhh." I faced Travis and Decker and ignored their half-smiles. "I'm as ready as I'm ever going to be." I held the sawn off, while Travis took Wraithbreaker from Frank. 

Travis shuffled his deck and slapped a card to his forehead, green arcs crackling over his eyes as the card disintegrated. Meanwhile, Decker kicked at the air in a dance, golden motes appearing around his own eyes, coalescing into a dull golden-yellow glow. I could see it, but I wasn't Witnessing. 

I closed my eyes and imagined the monsters we had faced. Their long, disgusting tentacles. It wasn't enough, Scry wasn't there. It was hiding from me, just out of view, behind me whenever I turned. 

Behind me.  
Tentacles.  
Wet, cold and sliding into my head or down my neck...  

"MMMM!" I covered my mouth as my body shuddered violently at the disgusting touch my mind showed me. Scry almost instantly pulled me to Witness and the world became bright and loud yet again. I put the hood on and followed toward the mouth of the cave. 

There wasn't much to see at first. A 7 foot tall and 6 feet wide stone corridor, irregular as if carved by water and expanded later by human hand. Assuming the frequent, dense grooves on the walls and ceiling were in fact, pick-marks. Decker went in first, while Travis held the rear. We all went slowly and quietly, avoiding loose stones and patches of gravel. I could see normally for the first twenty or thirty feet, but where darkness started, it was more like... knowing what I should be seeing. In vivid colors. We followed the singular corridor, going ever so slightly up, another fifty feet or so. We reached a cavern, round in shape, with only one other exit, stairs to the right, leading down. The walls of the room were carved into thirty or so horizontal niches, most home to a skeleton. 

_Burial ground. Let's respect the dead._ 

I took a deep breath and braced myself for what was to come. I tightened my grip on my weapon, if only to stop my hands shaking. We went down the long stairs, another burial chamber, even more skeletons. This time, at the bottom of the stairs, a weak light trembled. 

Decker moved us to the side and whispered. "It's time. The chamber down there is much bigger and has a central column. Behind it is the altar. I go left, you go right." He did another dance-cast, this time slowly, quietly. 

The golden spectral bear form I knew settled around me comfortably as Travis handed me Wraithbreaker and ammunition. 

McCoy drew his sword with a quiet clatter. "Shoot to kill." 

He stalked soundlessly down the stairs, our footsteps also quiet, but thunderous in comparison. The room was much bigger than those before, with many more interred dead. The central column was thick enough to house six just on the side we could see. The light was coming from somewhere behind the pillar, but reflected off crystals in the walls, reaching even the entrance. 

I followed Travis, gun in hands, quietly as I could. Finally, after long, long seconds I could see beyond the pillar. There was a stone dais and a large altar of stone. Behind it, another tunnel. 

There were no black monsters, but there was a man behind the altar. Tall, blonde, around maybe thirty. Extremely well dressed, like an aristocrat of some kind, golden ring on his finger, golden pocket watch chain connecting a button to his pocket. He was reading a book set on the altar, lit up and blinded by the lanterns illuminating the space. 

There was a sound like sails catching wind and Decker manifested in front and slightly above the man. Golden motes trailed from behind the pillar, ending in a swift sequence of extreme brutality. 

I watched, frozen and wide-eyed. This was not the McCoy I knew. 

First, the sword whistled in anger as it pierced the jugular and arced away, trailing blood. McCoy's boots hit the man's chest, knocking him over and crushing his ribs as they hit the ground. The sword turned from the arc and stabbed at the downed man thrice. Through the liver. Through a lung. Through the heart. The head rolled with the final swing. In a split second and fountains of blood, it was done. 

Or so I thought. 

I saw the man's soul, tattered and frayed at the edges rise from his mangled body, now quickly turning to dust. 

"Hello, Leonard." McCoy thrust his left hand into the soul and made a fist. "Not.Yet." The words fell with the cold, calm finality of a chopping block. 

I could see him let go. Of the chains. Of the burdens of regret. Of all but the thinnest restraint. The hatred on his face was impossible to look at. 

"You are not finished." McCoy stomped. "But you will be." 

His right hand started glowing brightly, forming into an enormous, angular array of sword-like golden claws. He moved the glowing points toward the soul's face, making it scream in agony, the walls echoing the torment. It wasn't a real sound, it skipped the ears to scratch right into your own soul. 

_This isn't right... You have no right... That's not... No... nonoNoNONONO._

_Breathe. Breathe._

"Decker!" I heard myself shout. "Stop!" 

He did, then turned his head slowly toward me, eyes lifeless and hollow. "He deserves it. All of it. And more." 

"Maybe! Maybe not! Who are you to judge?! This is too much!" I tried. 

"I am me, and I have six bones to pick with this one. Six names." He glanced at his victim. "It's. Fucking. Personal. He is NOT getting away. I WILL have my justice."

_He is talking. Talking is good. Not gone too far. Good. Breathe._

"This is not justice! This is vengeance! He is done, dead, he can't harm anyone anymore." 

"I don't care." 

_Breathe. Please, Decker, I know you're still you... And I'm sorry._

"Stop! You must stop! Please... For me..." My voice broke, but I pressed on. I had to. I invoked the oath. 

"You promised to stand where my father couldn't! My father would never do that! Please..." 

_Breathe._ 

"AAAAAAAAAARGGGHHHHHHHHH!" McCoy bellowed at the top of his lungs. The glow died. Decker's intensity dimmed from burning rage to menace. He stood there, breathing heavily. 

He turned to face the soul. "When you see The Lightbringer, you put in a good word for her, you hear? You know you owe her." 

Decker let go of his captive. It hovered there for a few moments, surprised. A split second later a flame edged document appeared underneath it. Flaming chains shot out and wrapped around the now panicked soul, pulling it into the paper. 

Then it was gone. 

Then silence. 

Then stillness. 

I felt a gaze on me. I met it.

Decker. I was ready. He wasn't angry. He wasn't smiling either. 

He Nodded.

And then we left.
